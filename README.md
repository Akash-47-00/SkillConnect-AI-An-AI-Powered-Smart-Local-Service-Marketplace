🤝 SkillConnect AI: An AI-Powered Smart Local Service Marketplace with Intelligent Provider Matching

SkillConnect AI is an AI-powered platform that connects customers with skilled professionals and local service providers. It allows users to describe their problems in natural language, and AI helps identify the required service category and skills before recommending suitable professionals.

Instead of manually searching through multiple service categories, users can simply describe what they need. SkillConnect AI understands the problem and helps them find the right professional.

Finding the right professional for a particular problem can be difficult. Customers may not know which type of service provider they need or where to find one.

SkillConnect AI solves this problem by allowing customers to describe their problems in natural language. Using the Google Gemini API, the system analyzes the description, identifies the likely service category and required skills, and recommends suitable professionals based on factors such as skills, location, ratings, experience, and availability.

The platform aims to make finding and booking local services easier, faster, and more convenient.

✨ Proposed Features (Key Features)

1. User Authentication
   
   - User registration and login.
   - Separate roles for customers, service providers, and administrators.

2. AI-Powered Problem Understanding
   
   - Customers describe their problems using natural language.
   - AI identifies the relevant service category and required skills.
   - Generates a short summary of the problem.
   - Planned support for Bangla and English.

3. Intelligent Professional Matching
   
   - Recommends suitable professionals based on customer requirements.
   - Matches providers using skills, location, ratings, experience, and availability.
   - Ranks recommendations based on matching scores.

4. Service Provider Management
   
   - Create and manage professional profiles.
   - Add skills, experience, and service categories.
   - Manage availability and booking requests.

5. Booking Management
   
   - Customers can request services from professionals.
   - Providers can accept or reject booking requests.
   - Users can track booking status and history.

6. Ratings and Reviews
   
   - Customers can rate completed services.
   - Submit reviews about professionals.
   - View provider ratings and feedback.

7. Admin Dashboard
   
   - Manage customers and service providers.
   - Manage service categories and skills.
   - Monitor bookings and reviews.
