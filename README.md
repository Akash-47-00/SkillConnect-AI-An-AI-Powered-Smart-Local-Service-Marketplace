🤝 SkillConnect AI: An AI-Powered Smart Local Service Marketplace with Intelligent Provider Matching

SkillConnect AI is an AI-powered platform that connects customers with skilled professionals and local service providers. It allows users to describe their problems in natural language, and AI helps identify the required service category and skills before recommending suitable professionals.

Instead of manually searching through multiple service categories, users can simply describe what they need. SkillConnect AI understands the problem and helps them find the right professional.
